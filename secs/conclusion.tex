\section{Conclusion and Discussion}
\vspace{-2mm}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We present automatic neural baking: an element-wise, order-independent ``texture'' on the mesh
surface that needs no UV atlas or depth sort and runs per fragment in real time. Next steps are
engine integration and element-level editing.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.} As in baking, we take a mesh and posed images, optimize per
scene, and bake the input illumination (static shadows included); relighting beyond swapping
environment maps (App.~\ref{app:scope}) is future work. Light from outside a near-convex part is
treated as distant, so strongly concave objects (\emph{toaster}) are hardest and nearby surroundings
can show parallax. Real captures (Sec.~\ref{sec:real}) cover three object-centric scenes.
